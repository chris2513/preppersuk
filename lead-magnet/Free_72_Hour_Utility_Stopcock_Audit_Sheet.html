72-Hour Home Utility & Stopcock Audit

Fifteen minutes. One pen. Know where every shut-off in your home is before a burst pipe, power cut or gas smell makes you hunt for it in the dark.

Tip: turn the stopcock off and on once. Many are stiff or seized. If yours won't move, ask a plumber to replace it before you need it. Tag each item with a bright label.

Step 2 // The five things that fail first

01 Gas boiler

Needs mains electricity for fan, pump and controls. No power usually means no heating or hot water.

Safety // read this
Smell gas? Don't use switches, flames or phones indoors. Open doors and windows, turn off the gas at the emergency valve if safe, leave the property and call 0800 111 999. Never burn charcoal, barbecues or camping stoves indoors: carbon monoxide kills.
